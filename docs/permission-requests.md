# Consigliere: permission requests and legal review

These are drafts, not sent messages or granted permissions. The owner confirmed on 6 October 2026 that permissions, legal review and market/social licences have not been obtained. Collection for Canada and Australia remains disabled. No request below accepts licence terms, fees or a contract.

## Canada: MPs, ministers and parliamentary secretaries

Recipient: **info@cie.parl.gc.ca**, listed on the [Commissioner’s contact page](https://www.ethicscanada.ca/en/contact-us).

Subject: Permission and data access request for public-registry research publication

Dear Office of the Conflict of Interest and Ethics Commissioner,

I am developing Consigliere, a publication that helps readers examine politicians’ publicly declared interests through source links, quoted evidence and clear limits. It does not recommend investments or allow readers to trade or copy a portfolio. A future subscription may support research features.

May Consigliere collect, store, structure, translate where necessary, and display information from your public registry, including Disclosure Summaries, Material Changes, Declarable Assets and published ministerial compliance measures? We would preserve the original language and source documents, distinguish declared interests from transactions, and provide a correction process.

Please confirm in writing whether these uses, including subscription-supported display and derived company/security matches, are permitted; any required attribution, retention or redistribution limits; and whether a bulk export or API is available. If permitted automated collection is the available route, please specify an appropriate request rate, caching policy and identifiable user agent.

We will not begin automated collection until the permitted scope is clear. Please direct this request to the appropriate data or licensing contact if necessary.

Thank you,
Consigliere project

## Canada: senators

Recipient: **cse-seo@sen.parl.gc.ca**, listed on the [Senate Ethics Officer’s contact page](https://seo-cse.sencanada.ca/en/contact/).

Subject: Permission and export request for the senators’ public registry

Dear Office of the Senate Ethics Officer,

I am developing Consigliere, a source-based research publication about politicians’ publicly declared interests. The publication may offer subscription-supported research features; it will not recommend investments or provide trading or portfolio-copying tools.

Please confirm whether we may collect, store, structure, translate, quote and display senators’ published disclosure summaries and subsequent public changes, with attribution and links to your registry. We would preserve source language and dates, show uncertainty in company matches, and offer corrections. We would not treat declarations as evidence of transactions or assign undisclosed dollar amounts.

Is a permitted export or API available? Otherwise, please specify whether automated retrieval is allowed and under what rates, caching, retention and reuse conditions. Please also clarify any restrictions on historical records and commercial or subscription-supported display.

We will wait for written clarification before collecting registry data.

Thank you,
Consigliere project

## Australia: House of Representatives

Recipient: **Members.Interests.Reps@aph.gov.au**, listed for the [Privileges and Members’ Interests committee](https://www.aph.gov.au/pmi/).

Subject: Request for permitted access and reuse of members’ interests records

Dear Registrar of Members’ Interests,

I am developing Consigliere, a source-based research publication about publicly declared political interests. I request permission to use members’ interests statements and alteration notices in a structured, attributed research view, including possible subscription-supported display.

The proposed process would retain the source PDFs, extract named shareholdings and dated additions or removals, link to the official statement, and send uncertain transcription or company matches for human review. We would distinguish declarations from trades, avoid undisclosed values, and provide corrections.

Could you supply a data export, or approve a documented method of automated access? Public automated access returned HTTP 403; we will not circumvent that response. Please confirm whether extraction, structuring, translation and commercial display are permitted notwithstanding any non-commercial or no-derivatives terms that apply to the website. Please specify attribution, retention, redistribution and access-rate conditions, and the office authorised to grant this permission.

We will not collect or publish these records until access and reuse permission are clear.

Thank you,
Consigliere project

## Questions for qualified counsel before release

Review the actual implementation and source agreements, including:

- House and Senate disclosure storage, analysis, citation and redistribution for this publication’s intended business model.
- UK Open Parliament Licence attribution and the treatment of personal information in public registers.
- Written Canadian and Australian permissions, including commercial use, derivatives, translations and retention.
- Impersonal portfolio estimates, annual anchoring, range propagation, missing-history labels and source coverage.
- Any paid alerts and wording. No instruction to buy, sell or rebalance, broker links or copy-trading functionality is included.
- Market and social-feed rights for storage, mobile display, redistribution and derived calculations. Personal-use access is not assumed sufficient.
- Any future hypothetical performance, benchmark or reaction calculations. No such figures are enabled now.

This list is a review brief, not legal advice or approval. Keep the signed review and permission documents outside source code; record their scope and expiry in the release record.
